# Ruhnn Holding Limited (如涵控股)

> Hangzhou-based Chinese influencer (KOL) incubation, marketing and e-commerce
> company. Ruhnn pioneered the "internet celebrity" (wanghong) commercialization
> model — signing and developing influencers, then monetizing their audiences
> through self-operated online stores on Taobao and Tmall and through a
> platform/advertising business that connects its KOL network with third-party
> brands. Listed on NASDAQ as RUHN in 2019; taken private in 2021. Tracked here
> as a portfolio company of Qiming Venture Partners.

## What Ruhnn operates

- ruhnn.com — corporate marketing site (Chinese; English mirror at /en/). A
  client-rendered React SPA with a catch-all route. Sections: about,
  marketing, advertising, co-branding, online retailers, star resources,
  star class, join us.
- www.kol18.com — 爱推广 ("Aituiguang"), the KOL/merchant platform operated
  under Ruhnn. Also a client-rendered SPA. End-user and merchant web app;
  no developer surface.
- Mobile: 爱推广商家 (Aituiguang Merchant) iOS app.
